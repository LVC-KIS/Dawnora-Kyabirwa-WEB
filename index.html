About Dawnora Kyabirwa

Dawnora Kyabirwa (D.K) is a grassroots nonprofit founded in Kyabirwa Village, Jinja, Uganda — located along the banks of the River Nile. We uplift orphans, disabled individuals, and vulnerable communities through healthcare, education, and rehabilitation.

The story behind it all

Why We Started

Kisige Elvis
Founder & Executive Director

“I grew up in a deep village along the River Nile. If you know that part of Uganda, you already know the weight that comes with it — diseases, poor education, drug abuse, broken families, and poverty that seem to have no end. That was my world.

After high school, I had no money for university. I worked as a housekeeper, saved, sacrificed, and eventually paid my own tuition. Today I study Computer Science. When our lecturer asked us to identify a problem in society and build a website around it, I had no question – I had lived the problem.

That assignment became this organization. Because sometimes a new dawn finds you first – and then calls you to be the light for others. I named it Dawnora – the aura of a new dawn – because after every darkness, the sun rises.”

Wakisima Ivan Blair
Co-founder & Community Director

“I was raised by a single mother in a life of constant struggle. Where I grew up, we did not grow – we survived. School fees were uncertain, food was never enough, and each day was a fight.

Yet my mother taught me something powerful: even when we had nothing, we could still share something. There were times I felt invisible, times I lost hope and wished someone would come and help us. No one came. And that is when I made a decision to become that hope for others.

Dawnora Kyabirwa was born from that journey. It exists to remind the hopeless that their story is not the end, and that even in the hardest life, a new dawn can still rise.”
