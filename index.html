Who we are

About Dawnora Kyabirwa

Dawnora Kyabirwa (D.K) is a nonprofit organization founded in Uganda, dedicated to uplifting orphans, disabled individuals, and vulnerable communities through healthcare, education, and rehabilitation support.

👁️

Our Vision

A Uganda — and ultimately a world — where every vulnerable person has access to dignity, opportunity, and the light of a new dawn, regardless of their circumstances.

🎯

Our Mission

To restore hope and transform lives by providing medical support, education, and rehabilitation to orphans, disabled individuals, and underserved communities along the Nile and beyond.

📍

Our Focus Area

We primarily serve communities in Eastern Uganda, with a vision to expand our reach nationally and, one day, across Africa and the world.

The story behind it all

Why We Started

I grew up in a deep village along the River Nile. If you know that part of Uganda, you already know the weight that comes with it — the diseases, the poor education, drug abuse, broken families, and poverty that seem to have no end. That was my world growing up.

After finishing high school, I had no money to join university. Not immediately. I had to sit for a few years and find work first. I got a housekeeping job — not glamorous, but it was a start. I saved. I sacrificed. And eventually, I began paying my own university tuition.

Today, I am studying for a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, and I am proud of every step it took to get here. During a module on Internet Technology and Web Design, our lecturer asked us to identify a problem in society and build a website around it. For me, there was no question. I had lived the problem.

That assignment became this organization. Because sometimes a new dawn finds you first — and then calls you to be the light for others. I named it Dawnora — the aura of a new dawn — because that is what has carried me this far. Not luck, not talent alone, but the promise that after every darkness, the sun rises.

And if a new dawn could reach me in that village by the Nile, I believe it can reach every story out there that feels hard but isn't impossible.

"After every darkness, the sun rises — and that sunrise is Dawnora."
F
KISIGE ELVIS
Founder, Dawnora Kyabirwa
CS Student · Community Advocate · Eastern Uganda

Our Co-founder

Wakisima Ivan Blair

My name is Wakisima Ivan Blair, and I was raised by a single mother in a life of constant struggle. Where I grew up, we did not grow — we survived. School fees were uncertain, food was never enough, and each day was a fight.

Yet my mother taught me something powerful: even when we had nothing, we could still share something.

There were times I felt invisible, times I lost hope and wished someone would come and help us. No one came. And that is when I made a decision to become that hope for others.

Dawnora Kyabirwa was born from that journey. It exists to remind the hopeless that their story is not the end, and that even in the hardest life, a new dawn can still rise.

"Even when we had nothing, we could still share the light of a new dawn."
IW
WAKISIMA IVAN BLAIR
Co-founder, Dawnora Kyabirwa
Community Builder · Advocate for the Hopeless
